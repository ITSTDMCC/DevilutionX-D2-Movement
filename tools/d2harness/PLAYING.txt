Diablo 1 with Diablo 2 movement (DevilutionX 1.5.3)
====================================================

Double-click devilutionx.exe.

- Click anywhere (or push the stick) to move straight to that exact point, Diablo 2 style.
- Pacing is Diablo 1's: in the dungeon you always walk at the original speed.
- In town you can run, like the original "Run in Town" jog. R toggles it (on by default);
  hold Left Ctrl to do the opposite for a moment.
- You slide past the corners of barrels, walls and other objects instead of stopping on them.
- Settings > Gameplay > "Diablo 2 Movement" off gives you stock Diablo 1 tile walking again
  (takes effect for the next game). "Diablo 2 Combat" is off; everything except movement is stock.
- While Diablo 2 movement is on, multiplayer games only see other players running this mod.
